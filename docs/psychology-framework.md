# Psychology Framework

The dossier combines three symbolic systems:

- Colours: emotional signal, aesthetic identity, attraction style, and shadow pattern.
- Animals: instinct sequence revealed as surface layer, self-image layer, and inner core layer.
- Temperament: A/B/C/D scoring mapped to Choleric, Phlegmatic, Melancholic, and Sanguine.

Compatibility ratings only belong in readings with two or more people.
